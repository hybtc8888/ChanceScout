# Research checklist

## Discover

Combine a topic, geography where supplied, current year, and words such as `open call`, `apply`, `deadline`, `报名`, `申报`, `征集` or `招募`. Search recent notices and older calls that may still be open. A publication lookback is not the same as an application window.

Useful themes:

| Developers | Creators |
|---|---|
| AI/agent hackathons, developer challenges, public bounties | AI short film, image, audio and design calls |
| Compute/cloud startup support, open-source grants | Creator incentives, commissions and partnerships |

Directories and social posts can reveal leads. Follow them to the organizer's site or an officially delegated rules/application page. Do not treat a page's claim to be official as sufficient when its identity is unclear.

## Verify fields, not a vague overall promise

1. **Identity:** named organizer, edition and a primary-source URL.
2. **Time:** registration and submission windows, source timezone, and any later closure notice. Do not turn the submission deadline into a new-registration deadline or supply a missing time yourself.
3. **Terms:** benefit type and conditions; personal/team/company and regional eligibility; fees, tool requirements, deliverable size and rights granted.
4. **Route:** open the published application route where possible. An email procedure or login requirement may be legitimate; say exactly what you checked. Do not register or send a test submission merely to verify a URL.

One authoritative primary source can establish facts. Independent corroboration adds confidence, but reposts do not count as independent. Resolve conflicting material terms or retain the item as an unverified lead.

When dynamic pages have incomplete readable text, another available reader or the public embedded page content may help. Preserve the source URL and what was actually read. A search snippet alone cannot establish the rules; blocked or missing facts remain unknown.

Use `official_verified` to mean the recorded facts came from a primary source, not that every clause, the user's eligibility, or a submission was verified. Use `corroborated` when independent reliable evidence also supports those facts, and `unverified` for unresolved core claims. Attach per-field evidence and limitations.

## Choose what to recommend

Rank by the user's requested topic, a feasible next action, time remaining, and benefit relative to effort. Large or competitive rewards may be included with their thresholds. Keep cash, credits, reimbursement, publicity and non-cash prizes distinct.

Never infer sensitive identity information or silently upload a personal profile. State relevant requirements instead. Separate confirmed upcoming windows and uncertain leads from currently open recommendations.
